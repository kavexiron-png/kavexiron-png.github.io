Our Story

A family table,
open to everyone

Bella Bistro began in 2014 when chef Marco De Luca and his sister Giulia set out to recreate the long, unhurried meals of their grandmother's kitchen in Puglia — handmade pasta, slow-braised ragùs, and vegetables sourced the same morning from the Saturday farmers' market.

Twelve years later, the same wood-fired oven still anchors the kitchen, our pasta is still rolled by hand every morning, and the restaurant has grown into a neighborhood gathering place — equally suited to a quiet anniversary dinner or a long Sunday lunch with the whole family.
