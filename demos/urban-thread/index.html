Our Story

Designed on rooftops.
Stitched for the block.

Urban Thread started in 2019 out of a two-bedroom apartment above a laundromat, screen-printing tees between shifts and selling them out of a duffel bag at local shows. No investors, no focus groups — just a crew of designers, skaters, and musicians who thought streetwear had gotten boring.

Today every piece is still designed in-house, cut in small batches, and tested by the same community that wears it. We work with independent mills for heavyweight cotton and recycled fibers, because longevity matters more than trend cycles. If it doesn't survive a winter on the train platform, it doesn't ship.
