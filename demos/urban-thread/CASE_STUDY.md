# Case Study: Urban Thread — D2C Streetwear Storefront

**Project type:** Conceptual / portfolio demo (fictional client, no real transactions)

## Client & Problem
Urban Thread is an emerging streetwear label that had built an engaged following through pop-up shows and social media, but was still selling exclusively via third-party marketplaces and in-person drops. They needed a direct-to-consumer storefront that captured their bold, rebellious brand identity, showcased limited drops, and gave visitors a frictionless path from "discover" to "add to cart" — without the overhead of a full platform build during early-stage validation.

## Solution
I designed and built a single-page, fully responsive storefront centered on conversion fundamentals: a high-impact hero with a clear CTA, a scannable 8-product featured grid with hover-driven product imagery, an authentic brand-story section to build trust, a UGC-style community grid to reinforce social proof, and a newsletter capture to retain visitors who aren't ready to buy. A sticky header with a live cart-count badge and toast-notification "add to cart" interaction gives the experience a modern, app-like feel despite being fully static.

## Tech Stack
- HTML5, Tailwind CSS (CDN) for rapid, consistent utility-first styling
- Custom CSS for component-level polish (product card hovers, reveal animations, toast/badge micro-interactions)
- Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks) for cart state, mobile nav, newsletter success state, and IntersectionObserver-driven scroll reveals
- Google Fonts (Space Grotesk + Inter) for a premium editorial/streetwear type pairing
- Unsplash photography for product and lifestyle imagery

## Result
As a conceptual launch site, Urban Thread demonstrates the kind of storefront that typically improves early D2C conversion by giving a young, mobile-first audience a fast, visually confident shopping experience — projected to meaningfully lift add-to-cart engagement and newsletter signups compared to a generic marketplace listing page. This build was delivered as a static, zero-dependency deliverable, making it trivial to host on any CDN or static host and iterate on quickly as the brand's catalog grows.
